About

Nobody should have to go it alone.

Life sometimes catches us off guard. When it does, it helps to know there are people nearby who'll pitch in. That's all this is: neighbors looking out for each other.

Free. We're neighbors, not a business.
For everyone. Every street, every household in Piney-Z, no matter who you are or how long you've lived here.
Just ask. Call or text (850) 508-3315, and we'll see what we can do.

Volunteer

Got a truck, tools, or simply hands? No special skills needed. Just reach out and let us know you're interested. If you do have special skills, please mention them, and we'll call you when something comes up that fits.

  • 💪 An extra pair of hands
  • 🛻 Have a truck
  • 🚗 Can drive
  • 🍲 Can cook
  • 🐕 Good with pets
  • 🔧 Handy
  • 💻 Tech
  • ☎️ Checking in on folks
Call or Text (850) 508-3315

Have one of these orange keychains? Keep it on your keys so the number is there when you need it.

Stay Close

No need to volunteer to be part of Piney-Z Help. Anyone can join our discussion forum / email list, where neighbors and volunteers talk things over: who has a truck free on Saturday, how a job went, ideas for the neighborhood. No commitment. Read along, and chime in whenever you like.
