Life sometimes catches us off guard. When it does, it helps to know there are people nearby who'll pitch in. That's all this is: neighbors looking out for each other.

Free. We're neighbors, not a business.

Stay in the loop.

When a neighbor could use a hand, the word goes out on our email list. You're welcome to join just to listen in. There's no commitment, and you can pitch in only when it suits you.

Join the email list

Joining online uses a Google account. Don't have one? Text Rick your email address and he'll add you.

Volunteer Signup

Become a Piney-Z Help volunteer. Let Rick know what you can help with, and he'll reach out when a neighbor needs exactly that. No special skills needed, just a willingness to show up.
